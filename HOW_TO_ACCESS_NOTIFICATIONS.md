# 🔔 How to Access Notification Settings in SnapBuy

## ✅ **Integration Complete!**

The notification settings are now available in your SnapBuy app through **two methods**:

### **Method 1: Store Settings Tab**

1. Go to any store in your app (`/store/{storeId}/overview`)
2. Look at the left sidebar navigation tabs
3. Click on the **Settings** tab (🛠️ settings icon)
4. You'll see the full notification settings interface

**Path:** `/store/{storeId}/settings`

### **Method 2: Header Menu**

1. Click the **3-dot menu** (⋮) in the top-right header
2. Look for **"Notification Settings"** option with bell icon (🔔)
3. Click it to open the settings as a popup

**Available when:** You're logged in and have a store selected

## 🔧 **Troubleshooting:**

**Not seeing the settings?**

1. Make sure you're logged in
2. Make sure you have a store selected
3. Navigate to `/store/{your-store-id}/settings`

**Notifications not working?**

1. Click "Enable" to grant browser permissions
2. Use the "Test" button to verify notifications work
3. Check your browser's notification settings

**Settings not saving?**

1. Check your internet connection
2. Ensure you have permission to edit the store
3. Try refreshing the page
